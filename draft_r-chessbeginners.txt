Title: Free Tool: Opening Repertoire Explorer by Rating Level (What Actually Works at 1400–2000+)

Body:

Hey everyone,

I'm sharing a tool I built because I think it solves a real problem I had as an improving player: "What openings should I actually be playing at my rating?"

**The Problem:**
You can memorize opening theory, but it doesn't match what's actually played and winning at your rating. A line that's solid for GMs can be a trap at 1600. I got stuck bouncing between openings until I realized I needed to see what *successful players at my exact rating* were actually playing.

**The Solution:**
I built a free opening-repertoire explorer powered by millions of real Lichess games. You pick your rating band (1400–1600, 1600–1800, 1800–2000, 2000+) and color, and it shows you:
- The top moves played by players at your level
- Win/draw/loss rates for each move (so you see which openings actually score)
- The continuation for your opponent's most common response (so you're not just memorizing blindly)

No login required. No ads. No sign-up. Just data.

**Link:** https://dylangerloski.github.io/ChessProject/

**Why this matters for improvers:**
- At 1600, you need ideas, not memorization (this tool shows you the ideas)
- You can see what's actually played, not what *should* be played
- You can compare your current rating to the next level up and see what opens get harder to play

I'd love your feedback—especially if you find mistakes in the data or have ideas for what would make this more useful for your rating band.
